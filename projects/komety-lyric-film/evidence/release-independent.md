## Boundary and processing limits

- No blanket shift, equal spacing, minimum duration, gate extrapolation, or release-to-next-onset clamp was applied. Several retained values already lie inside an ambiguous acoustic transition.
- Non-overlap must not determine a physical release. KOM-020 землёй interval extends across the next fricative onset 179.705, and several joined /d z/ intervals overlap the following onset. This establishes uncertainty, not proven independently sung overlap. If listening confirms real overlap, preserve it rather than force a gap.
- Raw waveform panels use decoded source and estimated-vocal samples without added filters. Band traces and spectrograms use Hann windows.512samples smear by roughly±5.8ms,1024 by±11.6ms,2048 by±23.2ms. The 1–3ms hop is not 1ms boundary accuracy.
- HTDemucs is noncausal and may leak instruments or extend/anticipate a vocal edge. Zero-lag clock alignment does not remove separation artifacts. A periodic stem tail alone cannot prove continuing sung phonation; original signal is the authority.
- Reverb can retain pitch/formants. Soft end-of-line sonorant candidates have wide intervals because signal inspection alone cannot cleanly distinguish continuing articulation from reflected vowel energy. No release includes the last nonzero sample or all residual reverberation.

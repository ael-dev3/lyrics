## Selected meanings

- **Тот, кто погас → The one who went dark.** Preserve the intransitive fading metaphor. Do not supply an unstated light, flame, beloved or death claim. `The one` is the complete substantive-pronoun equivalent of Тот; `went dark` is a single source verb's complete English expression. Кто, будет, ярче, светить, чем and кометы retain separate events. Natural English places shine before brighter, while focus follows the reversed source order.
- **Пролетающие над планетой → Passing over the planet.** The participle refers to the preceding comets. The necessary article shares планетой.
- **Из пустоты … Юности вольная птица.** Keep the full conditional/negative relation: without the addressee's beauty, youth's free bird will not be born from the void. The original inversion places the predicate before its subject. The displayed English keeps natural subject-before-predicate order: `From the void, without your beauty / youth's free bird will not be born.` Every source word remains independent. Не activates only not; родится activates the complete `will be born`. Твоей activates your and красоты activates beauty separately. Юности, вольная and птица retain separate subject anchors.
- **Лети / Свети.** Preserve distinct Fly and Shine imperatives. Над is above; землёй supplies the Earth, including its grammatical article. Planetary capitalization is consistent with the space imagery and later spoken Planet Earth.
- **Словно орёл / Словно огонь.** Keep both independently performed repetitions. An eagle requires an article sharing орёл; fire is a mass noun and does not need an article. Repeating a phrase does not turn two eagle or fire events into one broad focus.
- **Тот, кто терял, будет снова любить.** Keep both verbs objectless. `Has lost` expresses prior loss without naming what was lost; `will / love / again` remain separately anchored to будет / любить / снова. A guessed lost lover or new love object would change the text.
- **За рассветом близится вечное лето.** Beyond preserves the metaphor of what follows/passes the dawn. `Draws near` is the smallest complete English verb equivalent of близится. Eternal summer is used without an unnecessary article.
- **Ночь до зари, ты в душе береги свою птицу.** The first phrase is elliptical; `Through the night till dawn` completes its temporal sense. Through, the and night share Ночь rather than receiving invented sung times. `You keep` is an explicit-subject directive and preserves ты. Keep … safe expresses береги, even though the two target words flank the bird noun in English. `Your bird` preserves свою птицу without implying contrastive ownership with an extra own. `Your soul` is a natural-English grammatical part-whole completion grounded in the explicit addressee ты; its focus belongs to душе alone. This completion does not introduce an unstated beloved or independent timing event.
- **Не дай ей разбиться → Don't let it crash.** The pronoun refers to the bird. English it does not transfer the bird's Russian feminine grammatical gender to a human woman. Crash retains the flight/fall image in разбиться; no broken-heart object is supplied. The four source words and four English meanings remain distinct.

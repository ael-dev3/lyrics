# Oliver Tree — Joyride · v1.0.0

Complete cinematic English lyric films for YouTube and TikTok, with stable cream/coral color emphasis and the original music-video imagery.

- **YouTube:** 1920×1080 at 60 fps.
- **TikTok:** dedicated 1080×1920 composition at 60 fps.
- **Lyrics:** all 395 supplied words, 70 display cues and 323 word/short-group focuses.
- **Publishing kit:** landscape thumbnail, portrait 1200×1600 TikTok profile cover, concise titles/descriptions and optional English captions.
- **Production:** original source, editable code, fonts, analysis stems, timing decisions, review evidence and file hashes in the complete archive.

The full recording is retained. Original 25 fps footage keeps its source cadence while new graphics render at 60 fps. Timing uncertainty and the render-recovery record are documented; no perfect-sync or human-listening-review claim is made.

# sotode 外で — призрак

## Edition

This lyric edition preserves the [original music video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Psp5vt8BwoY), its complete narrative and soundtrack. Russian-led passages use Russian and English; Japanese-led passages use Japanese with original Russian and English translations. Concurrent voices keep independent centered reading blocks rather than clipping a backing phrase to the foreground vocal's ending.

Original-script lettering uses Noto Serif and Noto Serif JP. Stable word or grammatical-group focus, pale paper colors and a fixed measured spectrum match the source practical light. No pronunciation layer or generated replacement scenery is included. The classical observer's gender is left unstated in the Russian Japanese-verse translation; English expansions follow their documented source contributors.

Production credit: AI-assisted transcription review, translation, timing review and lyric design. Cover composition is code-native adaptation of original source frames; it introduces no generated replacement photography.

## Source credits and classical texts

Artist: **sotode 外で**. Title: **призрак**. The artist describes the group as a shoegaze band from Yekaterinburg.

Source-supplied video credits: directors dana arshagti & anna ananich; cinematographer fedor levshin; camera assistant pavel inx; cast marusya may, garold adventure, dasha geniyatova and roman muradov. The complete original end card is preserved.

- **赤染衛門 / Akazome Emon:** Ogura Hyakunin Isshu 59, Goshūi Wakashū, Love II, 680. The translation retains the counterfactual regret and past viewing of the lowering moon. [Museum text and attribution](https://www.samac.jp/search/poems_detail.php?id=59) · [All-Japan Karuta Association](https://www.karuta.or.jp/karuta-everyday/2911/).
- **壬生忠岑 / Mibu no Tadamine:** Ogura Hyakunin Isshu 30, Kokin Wakashū, Love III, 625. The performed lower-verse fragments are reordered; the comparative meaning is retained. [Museum text and attribution](https://www.samac.jp/search/poems_detail.php?id=30) · [All-Japan Karuta Association](https://www.karuta.or.jp/karuta-everyday/2795/).

These are source-text attributions, not claims that a modern published translation was reused. Russian and English meaning translations were prepared for this edition. Adapted third-party music and imagery remain subject to their owners' rights; the repository's software/documentation license does not grant reuse rights over them.

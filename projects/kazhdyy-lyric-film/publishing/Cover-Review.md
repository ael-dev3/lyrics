# Cover review

Both covers use the current song's source illustration as their identity reference and ink, ivory and vermilion as their base color direction. They are promotional AI-assisted adaptations, separate from the approved film imagery. The built-in image-generation tool created the selected originals; exact initial and crop-safety prompts are preserved in `Cover-Source`.

The full Cyrillic title and artist were inspected on both generated originals. A second pass increased edge clearance. The final YouTube cover is 1920×1080; TikTok is portrait 1200×1600, 3:4 width:height. They were uniformly resized with Lanczos and negligible edge cropping, without stretching. JPEG export uses square pixels and has no EXIF orientation dependency. Generated detail originates at the selected-source dimensions recorded in the manifest.

The 150×200 TikTok view preserves the title, artist and two figures. Both covers preserve the title, artist, woman's face and red eyes under a 5%-per-edge crop. Native, 300×400 portrait and 320×180 landscape views were inspected. These are local crop simulations; no actual upload preview or platform posting is claimed.

The adapted source imagery remains subject to the repository's third-party rights exclusions. Publishing copy credits the source and discloses AI assistance.

## Translation and complete focus

English is authored for this edition, with equal font, weight, resting/focus colors and timing precision. Its word order can differ from Russian. Every target token has source ownership and a rationale; required English grammar never receives invented independent syllable timestamps.

- «Засыпай» owns both **Fall asleep**; «на / моих / плечах» remain individual **on / my / shoulders** events.
- Two inverted source lines about the winds form one complete reading cue, allowing English to keep the actual subject: **The fleeting winds will steal you and me away in dreams**. Its source-linked English focus legitimately moves backward.
- «Непрожитые впрок года» retains the sense of unlived years saved for later; this shares a complete two-line sentence with the preceding «Исчезают…».
- «Пусть ночь озарит рассвет» is interpreted as **Let dawn light up the night**. The forms permit another grammatical reading; this is a documented editorial interpretation, not a claim that the alternative is impossible.
- «Ничего нет» uses **There is nothing**. Russian negative concord needs a complete English negative existential: **nothing** follows «Ничего» and remains focused over «нет», while **There is** joins at «нет». Focusing affirmative **There is** alone during the held negation would communicate the wrong meaning.
- «Не…ни» supplies **no** by the exact union of two separate source events, never an enclosing span across unrelated words. **Will leave no trace of them** keeps the future and the clues as the referent.
- Passive «согрет» supplies **are warmed by**; **you / my / warmth** keep their individually corresponding words.

Lexical release, neutral complete-line hold and fade are independent. Continuous vocal handoffs replace a cue at full incoming opacity. Longer gaps allow a neutral reading hold and a slow fade; held singing is never faded merely because a model stopped emitting characters.

## Reusable decisions from this edition

- Derive the palette from the source photograph before adding chromatic accents. Here neutral silver fits the white bulbs and paper objects better than the earlier warm highlight.
- A quiet arrangement can support one narrow measured trace along a real material edge. Preserve a fixed anchor, small tapered travel, smooth release and readable words; additional light effects are not a quality requirement.
- Keep long vowels separate from sparse model character cores and from neutral reading holds. Inspect silence on both sides: avoiding premature release must not create focus over an instrumental gap.
- Inspect complete translated meaning through every state. A held negative needs the full English negative construction, even when necessary English grammar has no separate Russian syllable.
- Verify states that actually exist. A token that enters focused and clears directly at a vocal handoff has no neutral lifetime; test its focused and cleared pixels rather than fabricating a neutral interval.
- Preserve the full source audio tail and hold the last real picture. Native video duration and decoded audio duration are separate limits.
- Review covers at their smallest intended display size. The tall artist line was raised before acceptance to retain the lower safe margin.

# Rose paper film lessons

## Let the artwork establish the material and palette

The original album image is visually static. Presenting it as a slightly tilted print on warm rose paper gives it a deliberate setting without inventing source-video movement. A pale sleeve edge, soft offset shadow and fine stationary texture support the image. The selected treatment keeps the photograph stable through the complete recording.

The paper and berry colors belong to this artwork. Equal dark Russian and English text retains a clear reading area, while one shared berry accent marks the current meaning. This edition does not turn a successful palette or rotation into a default for other songs: choose their materials, color and motion from their own imagery and arrangement.

## A continuous ribbon can carry restrained musical motion

A single smooth ribbon provides an alternative to a row of spectrum bars. Its 64 measured frequency bands use immediate feature attack, a short exponential display release, neighboring-band smoothing and bounded section-dependent height. The camera, album print and glyph positions remain fixed. Stronger passages gain measured reach without overwhelming the text.

The ribbon's sinusoidal spatial profile is designed geometry; it is neither a physical waveform nor a vocal-pitch trace. Its frame-index response uses current and past features, but the underlying zero-phase filtering and centered analysis windows include nearby future samples. Preserve this distinction when describing synchronization. Audio features, source-word events, semantic focus and cue visibility share a clock without becoming interchangeable measurements.

## Refine translations before broadening focus

Complete English meanings must receive complete highlights. «Вслух / out loud», «улетай / fly away» and «умоляю / am begging» retain their full expansions on the corresponding source event. Explicit subjects, objects, conjunctions and negation remain separate when independently sung.

The instrumental construction «жарким огнём» does not contain a separate preposition. English still needs “with”: anchoring **with blazing** to «жарким» and **fire** to «огнём» completes the meaning and permits a natural handoff without an invented timestamp. Attaching “with” only to the final noun created a disconnected focus state.

Natural wording can also enable finer correspondence. **Those same** preserves the demonstrative in «Те же» and allows each word to follow its own event. The wider word requires a fresh geometry check in both layouts. Conversely, **My untamed love** and **burning the heart** keep natural English order even when their highlights move backward. Never distort meaning merely to make every focus move left to right.

A full scan covers every repeated pattern and both language lanes. Independent semantic fixtures should reject previous incomplete and over-broad mappings; copying generated indices into expected values would miss editorial defects.

## Keep evidence and approval distinct

Bounded alignment, independent models, vocal-stem traces and retained timing candidates support word-level preparation. They do not prove performed coverage or perceptual boundaries. In particular, short coarticulated conjunctions and sustained releases require listening; a one-frame neutral gap cannot be repaired solely because it looks unusual in data.

The current recording received comprehensive human review and explicit production authorization bound to the frozen preview. The evidence identifies that reviewer role without inventing automated listening telemetry. The renderer and final-file checks must preserve those exact inputs, original audio, all intended visual layers and paired focus. A changed renderer requires its own visual and encoded-output checks even if the reviewed composition stays unchanged.

## Compare a new rasterizer against the actual preview

A shared SVG source does not guarantee that two rasterizers display it identically. Native-size browser comparison caught omitted letter spacing and opacity in the smaller artist/title/album labels. Production parses those attributes from the frozen scene and applies them through native canvas text drawing. Original artwork also needs explicit placement because the SVG decoder omits embedded raster images. No reviewed scene input changes for these adapters.

The cached compositor is compared against full shared-scene rasterization with the same documented adapters. That numerical comparison supports code-path equivalence; it is not browser pixel equality. Separately inspect the native browser composition, corrected stills and encoded diagnostics, then bind the adopted renderer hashes to the approved input identity.

When streaming native canvas buffers to an encoder, wait for each write callback before the next paint. The native allocation can be reused; accepting a stream write does not prove the bytes have been consumed. Retain one static surface and the current text surface instead of writing a large per-frame image cache.

## Preserve the complete composition and prepare real upload assets

The review screen includes the full recording, original artwork, both lyric rows and the ribbon in both aspect ratios. Diagnostic views and isolated text checks supplement that complete preview. A successful audio seek or an image preload does not establish visible picture presence; inspect the actual composition after load, seeking and format changes.

Keep the 16:9 and 9:16 films as separate compositions. Upload covers are separate poster layouts with the complete artist and title, including the established 1200×1600 portrait profile cover. Inspect small-size and crop proofs, then hash the delivered copies. Final checks distinguish exact frame and audio integrity from human perception; neither replaces the other.

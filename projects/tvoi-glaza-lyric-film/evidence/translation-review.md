## Editorial decisions

- «И встретил в них тебя»: “And found you in them.” Preserve the poetic image of encountering the beloved in the eyes. No new location or love object is supplied.
- «Как первый луч»: “Like the first ray of light.” “of light” completes the English sense of ray and shares that source noun's event. Ordinal articles share their translated ordinal.
- «Ты словно в небесах заря»: “You are like the dawn in the heavens.” Natural English order is retained; dawn highlights when the final Russian noun occurs. “are” supplies the zero copula and shares “You”.
- «Ты полная Луна»: “You are the full Moon.” English retains the subject, fullness and lunar image.
- «А дальше пустота»: “And after that, emptiness.” The temporal completion belongs to «дальше». No added verb, addressee or causal explanation.
- «Сотни ночных дорог напомнят мне тебя»: “Hundreds of night roads will remind me of you.” Both required English prepositions and the future auxiliary are highlighted as parts of their complete lexical events. “me” and “you” remain separate.
- «Я в них терял свою любовь, и заново встречал»: “On them I lost my love, and found it again.” “it” resumes the explicitly stated love; it does not add another beloved. “found it” shares «встречал»; “again” separately follows «заново». English past tense retains the narrated losses and renewed encounters; the repeated context carries their iterative sense.
- «Лишь дай мне посмотреть в последний раз в твои глаза»: “Just let me look into your eyes for the last time.” Keep each preposition, last, time, your and eyes separately linked. No whole-clause highlight is used to avoid reordered English.
- «И стук колёс как эшафот»: “And the clatter of wheels, like a scaffold.” “scaffold” means the execution platform in this metaphor, not construction scaffolding. The following line continues the same sentence.
- «Я выберусь, моя любовь»: “I will get out, my love.” Preserve the future and escape meaning without inventing a named prison or destination.
- «Тебе я в след кричал»: “I cried out after you.” The supplied «в след» spelling represents the single adverbial construction «вслед»; this is the one documented multi-source mapping, to “after.” Its focus uses the union of the two token intervals and releases during any intervening gap. It is not a pronunciation aid.
- «Я научусь тебя любить»: “I will learn to love you.” “will learn” and “to love” are complete English grammatical spans, while “you” follows its own source event despite reordered display position.
- «Мне без тебя не жить»: “I cannot live without you.” “I” follows dative «мне»; “cannot” follows «не» under the impersonal infinitive construction's modal meaning. Without, you and live remain independent.
- «Твой голос мой ориентир»: “Your voice is my guide.” The omitted copula belongs to the English predicate's “is my” event; your, voice and guide remain separate.
- «Я сам себя казнил»: “I was the one who executed myself.” “was the one who” preserves emphatic «сам»; “myself” corresponds to reflexive «себя»; “executed” corresponds to «казнил». This retains the punitive metaphor without replacing it with a softer feeling or a new event.
- «Я солнцем обернусь и в них останусь навсегда»: “I will turn into the sun and stay in them forever.” Natural reading order is preserved. The sun, transformation, conjunction, location, staying and forever have independent source links; the final English future is carried by the first coordinated future predicate.

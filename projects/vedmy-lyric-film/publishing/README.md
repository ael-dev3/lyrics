# Publishing assets

The posting kit contains native landscape and portrait videos, separate platform titles/descriptions, a YouTube thumbnail and a portrait TikTok profile cover. Optional Russian, English and bilingual SRT captions are line-level companions; the videos already contain word highlighting.

Covers use the original release photograph, the approved warm autumn colors and Cormorant Garamond. Typography and layout are code-defined in `src/CoverRoot.tsx`, exported through Chromium as RGB JPEGs. YouTube: 1920×1080. TikTok: 1200×1600 portrait (3:4 width:height).

The cover review includes YouTube at 320 pixels wide, TikTok at 150×200 and 300×400, and a 5% edge-crop simulation. Full title, artist and central figures remain recognizable. This is a local crop review, not a platform upload test.

Music and original release artwork: BALKON. The supplied release metadata does not identify the photographer. Credits, source link and AI-assisted lyric preparation disclosure are included in the publishing copy. Source imagery remains subject to its creators' rights.

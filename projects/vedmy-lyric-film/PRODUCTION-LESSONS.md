# Woodland preview lessons

## Keep the scene continuous

The selected treatment uses one fixed autumn palette. Continuous environmental color supports the photographed setting; conspicuous palette changes made the presentation feel more designed than inhabited. Decorative corner branches and an extra atmospheric glow were also removed. Sparse falling leaves retain the nature motif without forming an ornamental frame.

Natural imagery does not require an organic shape for every element. Opposing rows of leaf-shaped spectrum elements created unintended facial associations. The measured visualizer now uses a small, single-sided straight spectrum. Vegetation belongs to the scene; sound measurement stays visually simple.

These are reusable selection principles, not a universal ban on color transitions or botanical geometry: judge each effect in motion and at small display sizes, and remove it when its most immediate visual reading conflicts with the song.

## Make motion follow sound without shaking the photograph

Leaf drift derives from measured air-band energy in the original mix, with a slower release than attack. Integrating velocity produces a continuous trajectory when the music changes; multiplying position directly by changing energy would cause jumps. Seeded lifetime offsets, size, tumble and vein detail avoid an identical particle procession. Leaves fade over the reading zone and recede over central figures. The photograph and text geometry remain fixed.

The audio-derived control is an artistic interpretation, not a detector of individual forest sounds or a physical simulation. The displayed spectrum retains 64 distinct measured frequency bands. Source timings, complete meaning focus, cue visibility, motion and the artistic strength envelope remain separate on one recording clock.

## Review sustained vowels independently

An aligner can place the onset convincingly while ending a sung vowel too soon. Here, several **На**, **стоило** and **утони** events needed explicit continuation candidates. Stem energy and periodicity support those proposals, but reverb and separation artifacts prevent treating them as listening proof. Each repeated performance retains its own events and candidates.

Full-track ASR also attached words to instrumental gaps and hallucinated an unrelated subtitle credit near the ending. Known text, bounded windows, independent methods and the actual recording must be reconciled; a forced alignment cannot prove that supplied wording was performed.

Test sensitivity to window boundaries and stem separation. A short negation moved by more than half a second in isolated-vocal estimates while its original-mix candidate stayed stable across windows. Conversely, expanding a section could pull its final word into the next phrase. Preserve all candidates, adopt changes individually with a reason, and reject zero-duration events and silence-spanning model spans as timing authority. A larger recognition model does not remove these failure modes.

Playback diagnostics must reset their cadence baseline after pause, seek and speed changes. Otherwise, a deliberate pause can be reported as a dropped-frame interval. Separate actual media-clock agreement from acoustic-boundary confidence and from unmeasured hardware latency.

## Preserve meaning and preview completeness

The discontinuous idiom **в глаза кидал … пыль** maps to a complete English meaning while **мне / me** stays independent. Its focus uses the union of actual source events and releases during the separately sung recipient. Explicit negations and possessives also retain independent correspondence. Grammatical completions receive the source event of the meaning they complete.

The full preview always includes the original artwork, animated leaves, spectrum, lyrics and complete soundtrack. Native stills supplement a playable preview; they do not replace it or establish synchronization approval. Browser glyph bounds caught a narrow right margin and insufficient portrait row spacing before handoff. Technical checks and selected visual inspections remain distinct from complete actual-audio review.

Place M4A metadata before media data with a lossless `-c:a copy -movflags +faststart` remux. A valid range-capable server alone did not prevent stalled metadata loading in the in-app player. After moving the metadata, the requested seek and playback initialized normally; AAC packet payloads and decoded PCM remained identical to the original recording. Verify actual media readiness and visible highlights after a fresh load, not merely successful HTTP responses.

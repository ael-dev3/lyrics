# TAKE ME THERE — production method

## Reviewed inputs and scope

The [synchronization review](evidence/sync-review.json) and [render authorization](evidence/render-authorization.json) bind the source-integrated preview to exact media, font, timeline, feature, scene, player and transcript hashes. The project owner attested full normal-speed review, reduced-speed review of uncertain words and held endings, and inspection of both layouts before authorizing the two full-length exports. This is an owner review record; the automated transcription comparisons remain fallible and do not become independent listening evidence.

The [preview timing audit](evidence/first-verse-sync-audit.json) corrected the early “far” focus near 19 seconds and related word boundaries before approval. The current map has 37 cues and 225 individually timed events. The original moving video is the picture source in both formats; the 9:16 version uses the approved shot-specific crop. The primary spectrum remains in one fixed position per format while existing windows, stars and reflections provide secondary musical response.

## Preview-to-master renderer

[`scripts/render-production.ts`](scripts/render-production.ts) calls the approval gate before frame capture or encoding. FFmpeg decodes the locked H.264 source in original 60 fps order. The production host then draws the unchanged `src/scene.ts` on each decoded picture at `frame / 60`, using the same timeline, measured features, shot map and original Space Grotesk font. It streams RGBA frames to FFmpeg for H.264 delivery and copies the original AAC packets. The source has 8,093 picture frames; the last source frame is held for three additional output frames to cover its 47.8 ms original audio tail. Both exports contain 8,096 frames at 60 fps.

The approved font is variable. Native canvas initially selected a light default face, so the production canvas explicitly sets its existing `wght` axis to 700, matching the browser’s CSS `700`. No font file or approved scene input was changed. [Three source-frame comparisons](evidence/render-parity.json) show small RGB differences between native diagnostic stills and saved browser stills at 19.15 s landscape, 74.05 s portrait and 97.30 s landscape. These are sampled visual parity checks, not a claim that every encoded pixel equals a browser screenshot.

The encoded H.264 stream receives explicit BT.709 color, TV range and square-pixel metadata through a stream-copy finalization. This does not re-encode picture or audio. Keep the finalization receipts and verify the resulting files, rather than treating encoder success as delivery verification.

## Final checks and posting assets

The independent [final-file verification](evidence/final-verification.json) passed in both formats: full strict decode, every frame timestamp, dimensions, color metadata, original AAC packet and decoded PCM identity, source-picture continuity and no unexpected black intervals. The last dark frames are present in the source ending. The [encoded landscape word-focus audit](evidence/encoded-focus-landscape.json) and [portrait counterpart](evidence/encoded-focus-portrait.json) each checked one actual decoded frame for all 225 timed events and 921 visible word-slot states; neither found a mismatch or ambiguous glyph-color result. Deliberately wrong focus targets were detected in 219 of 225 events per format ([landscape control](evidence/encoded-focus-landscape-negative-control.json), [portrait control](evidence/encoded-focus-portrait-negative-control.json)); six one-slot cases have no alternative target. These checks establish delivery integrity and selected visual focus; they do not independently certify sung phonemes or every transition frame.

The [publishing folder](publishing/README.md) records the two source-frame covers and platform copy. [`scripts/package-delivery.ts`](scripts/package-delivery.ts) checked the verified film hashes, copied the two films and platform assets into a new local upload kit, added optional English SRT/VTT captions, and wrote a manifest and SHA-256 checksums. All listed package files passed checksum validation; probes of both copied videos returned the intended dimensions, 60 fps, 8,096 frames and BT.709 tags. The [delivery receipt](evidence/delivery-receipt.json) identifies the checked masters and Desktop copy without publishing large media files in Git. A later material change to the lyrics, source, scene or layout needs a new complete preview review and authorization before another production run.

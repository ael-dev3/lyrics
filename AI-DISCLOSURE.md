# AI use disclosure

This is an **AI-assisted lyric-film and workflow project**. It should not be described as entirely hand-coded, manually timed or hand-illustrated.

## Joyride production

The Joyride landscape and portrait films were developed with **OpenAI Codex (GPT-6 Astra)**. Assistance covered TypeScript implementation, comparison of audio-model observations, sample-timing decisions, waveform and visual inspection, publishing copy and documentation. Stored evidence distinguishes model estimates and display rounding from proven acoustic boundaries; no human/native-listener audition is claimed.

The original Oliver Tree music video and full recording are retained. Original footage remains 25 fps while added graphics run at 60 fps. Separate covers use built-in image generation with a source frame as reference; its underlying model version was not exposed. No synthetic singing replaces the performance. ## How AI was used

- **OpenAI Codex / language-model assistance:** substantial code authoring, workflow design and documentation, troubleshooting, translation drafting, timing reconciliation, visual-layout development, automated checks and publishing copy.
- **Whisper-family speech models and stable-ts:** transcription and bounded multilingual alignment observations. These observations were reconciled with waveform offsets and repeated-performance evidence; they are not ground-truth lyric timings.
- **Demucs:** separation of mixed audio into vocal and instrumental components for analysis. The stems are derivatives of the source recording, not new original performances.
- **TorchAudio MMS-FA with uroman:** an independent multilingual alignment pass for Отменяй, compared with windowed Whisper observations and acoustic evidence. Model scores and agreement are not calibrated ground-truth confidence.
- **Built-in image generation:** YouTube and TikTok cover artwork was generated/adapted from source-film imagery and then resized for delivery. Available TikTok prompts and cover variants are preserved in [the publishing material](projects/roi-adore-rebuild/tiktok/README.md). The covers are AI-assisted adaptations, not unaltered screenshots or wholly original character designs.
- **Source preprocessing:** the selected Roi 1080p60 upload was described in the acquisition record as YouTube's upscaled derivative of native 720p60 material. We did not create missing native animation detail.

## Human direction and technical processing

Ael selected the music, provided lyric references, set the creative direction, requested revisions and accepted the resulting films. Remotion and FFmpeg performed deterministic rendering and encoding; the measured visualizer was generated by explicit DSP code and checked with calibration tests. Source recordings and original animation were retained in the films. Their existence and authorship are not attributed to AI or to this project.

The Roi rebuild and Отменяй reviews combined automated checks and visual inspection. They do not claim independent human-listening certification of every word boundary. Overlapping vocals and translated semantic groupings retain uncertainty. Numerical frame-rounding bounds and DSP calibration do not establish perfect transcription, translation or musical interpretation.

The Отменяй production task used **OpenAI Codex with GPT-6 Astra**, confirmed from local task model metadata. Its separate image-generation tool did not expose an underlying image-model version. The source illustration is retained as adapted artwork with new motion; the 60 fps export is not a claim that the source illustration contained native 60 fps animation.

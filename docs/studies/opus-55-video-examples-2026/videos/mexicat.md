## Distinctive visual grammar

- **Palette and density:** Almost black, chalk-white, and a narrow hot-orange accent dominate. Cream paper is held for one sequence so it has event value. Fine grids, circles, vectors, crosshairs, and tiny labels make the piece feel like a scientific instrument or forensic display.
- **Type hierarchy:** One very large statement or word drives each shot. Much smaller monospace labels imply measurements and context. Fake prompt boxes, confidence bars, graph axes, and source-code labels give the lyric an in-world container.
- **Motion and camera:** The sampled images show push-ins through wire tunnels, perspective distortion of grids, growing numeric displays, and horizontal/vertical wipes or occlusion bars. A handful of rendered subjects punctuate the abstract system. Exact in-between movement remains to be checked against the full frame index or playback.
- **Transition rhythm:** Palette inversion at about 67 s and repeated prompt-response reveals divide the two halves. Scene-score spikes cluster around 23 s, 59–69 s, 118–129 s, and 140–156 s; those are candidates for close transition inspection, not a definitive cut list.
- **Continuity devices:** The orange accent, registration marks, probability panels, and repeated terminal-like input prompt tie together scenes that otherwise use different illustration methods. The P(doom) motif becomes a numerical climax.
- **Readable text:** Large display terms remain legible at the native 1280 × 720 source. Microtype and some secondary data fields are ornamental at contact-sheet scale; do not treat them as verified factual data without full-resolution review.

## Useful production lessons

1. A narrow accent color can support a long-form piece when one neutral backdrop and one exceptional palette reversal are deliberately timed.
2. Lyrics can inhabit diagrams, prompt fields, forms, graphs, and numerical readouts; each surface changes the *meaning* of the line rather than simply displaying captions.
3. Repeated input/response structures make rapid stylistic changes feel like chapters of one story.
4. Reserve the largest figurative reveals and largest type for chorus or argument pivots, then return to small technical detail to restore scale.
5. The ending pays off recurring UI language with a final interface gag instead of an unrelated logo or fade.
